Tango Puzzle

Fill the 6×6 grid with Fire (🔥) and Ice (🧊) symbols.

Rules: 1. Each row and column must have exactly 3 of each symbol.
2. No three identical symbols in a row (horizontally or vertically).
3. Match (=) means adjacent cells are the same. Cross (×) means they are different.
